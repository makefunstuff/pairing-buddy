## Made for this project

These are this project's own and under its MIT License, listed here so nobody has to wonder where they came from:

- **The narration** of the tutorial video (every MP3 in `docs/video/audio/` but `music.mp3` and `sfx-*.mp3`, and the
  voice in `site/public/media/hero.*`) is text-to-speech from ElevenLabs (Eleven v4, the premade voice "Daniel"),
  made through OpenRouter's paid API. ElevenLabs' terms leave the rights in the output with the user, allow commercial
  use on paid access, and ask for no attribution; using the audio must still follow ElevenLabs' prohibited-use
  policy.
- **The music bed** (`docs/video/audio/music.mp3`, also under the voice in `site/public/media/hero.*`) was generated by
  Google's Lyria 3 through OpenRouter; Google claims no ownership of generated output. The file may carry Google's
  SynthID audio watermark.
- **The blips** (`docs/video/audio/sfx-*.mp3`) and **the earcons** (`sounds/*.wav`, also dedicated to the public
  domain under CC0) are square and sine waves made with FFmpeg by `docs/video/sfx.sh` and `sounds/make.sh`.
- **The sprites, favicon and icons** (`site/public/sprites/`, `site/public/*.png`, `favicon.svg`) are drawn as pixel
  grids in `site/sprites/build.mjs`. Their palette is PICO-8's 16 colours.
- **The captures, stills and clips** (`docs/images/`, `docs/video/clips/`, `docs/video/stills/`, `site/public/media/`)
  are screen recordings of Pair itself, of the sample projects in `docs/captures/`, and of the questions spoken by
  espeak-ng for the voice scene; the words in them are the project's or a model's.

Generated speech and music may have limited copyright protection where you live; the MIT License grants whatever
rights the project holds in them.
